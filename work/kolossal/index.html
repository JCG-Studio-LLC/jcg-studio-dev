Brand

A first impression for Kolossal

I built the brand around the colossal squid, giving a new ocean exploration and conservation nonprofit a way to introduce itself and its quest to find and film the animal.

01 · Logo

The squid beside the name

I paired a coral orange squid silhouette with tall, condensed capitals. The same animal appears at the end of the website’s dive.

02 · Colour

From Antarctic sky to depth

I used pale sky, ink blue and water shifting from light teal to black, with coral orange taken from the squid.

03 · Type

Headlines, reading and depth

I used Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for headlines, Newsreader for reading, and DM Mono for small labels and depth readings.

04 · Illustration

Drawn in code, in motion

I used animated, code-drawn illustrations throughout: jellyfish, an iceberg, toothfish, the ACKBAR camera trap and the colossal squid.

Website

A dive through the website

I designed the site as one long descent from the surface to 2,000 m, with darkening water and a depth reading following the scroll. At the bottom, a lamp lights the colossal squid.
